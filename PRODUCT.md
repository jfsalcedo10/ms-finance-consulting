# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses, individuals, and foreign/expat residents or business owners in
Cartagena, Colombia who need accounting, tax, or financial consulting —
plus a secondary audience seeking modern data analysis / data-driven
financial insight. Cartagena's tourism and expat presence means the
audience is genuinely bilingual (Spanish and English), not just
Spanish-speaking locals with an English site as an afterthought.

## Product Purpose

M&S Finance Consulting exists to give clients in Cartagena trustworthy,
personal accounting, tax, and financial consulting — backed by 30+ years
of hands-on practice — now paired with modern data analysis capability, so
clients get both established financial expertise and current data-driven
insight from a single dependable relationship.

## Positioning

A father-son practice where clients always work directly with the same
named, credentialed professional — never handed to a junior or a call
center. The combination of a formally credentialed accountant (30+ years,
specialization) and a formally credentialed data scientist under one
practice is the differentiator: most solo/small accounting practices in
Cartagena offer one or the other, not both, and not from people the client
can actually reach directly.

## Operating Context

Client engagements center on bookkeeping, tax filing/planning, financial
statement preparation, and ad hoc financial or data-analysis consulting
conversations. Site visitors reach out via the contact form or the direct
contact details (email, phone/WhatsApp) in the footer — the form submits to
Web3Forms, a no-backend email-delivery service, and needs only a real
access key (see README) to go live.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fully bilingual (Spanish default, English available via a language
  switcher) — not optional, given the expat/tourism client base.
- Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build tooling, must remain openable
  via `file://` or a trivial static server.
- Contact form submits to Web3Forms; needs a real access key from
  web3forms.com before it will actually deliver messages (placeholder
  `WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY` in `contact.html`).
- Full physical street address is not yet finalized — city-only
  ("Cartagena, Colombia") is used until provided.

## Product Principles

1. Direct, personal relationship over impersonal scale — every client
   works with the actual named professional, never a junior or a call
   center.
2. Decades of financial trust paired with modern data capability — the
   two generations' combined credentials (accounting + data science) are
   the real differentiator, not a tech feature bolted onto a traditional
   practice.
3. Bilingual by default — Spanish and English both need to feel like the
   site's native language, reflecting Cartagena's actual client base.
4. Never invent evidence — no fabricated testimonials, certifications, or
   client logos; only confirmed, real facts appear on the site.
